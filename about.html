A little about us

Here for the everyday moments.

M.A. Victory Market is a neighborhood shop owned by Mercy Ampomah, serving families and neighbours in Oyibi with foodstuff and provisions.

From local ingredients for home-cooked meals to pantry essentials and useful household goods, our aim is to make everyday shopping simple and welcoming.

We are located at Old Ayensu Estates. Stop by, or call and message us to ask about what is in stock.
